## Hacktoberfest 2025: Contribute to NeoPilot and Win Exclusive Swag!

This October, every contributor who gets a PR merged will receive an official **NeoPilot Hacktoberfest Certificate**.  

And that’s not all — the **Top 5 projects** will get to pick from our exclusive swag list 🎁

Please show your support and give a ⭐ to the [NeoPilot repository](https://github.com/neopilot-ai/neoai-ide).  
👉 Don’t forget to follow us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/neopilot/) and [Twitter/X](https://x.com/NeoPilot) for updates, highlights, and winner announcements.  

---

## How to Participate in 4 Easy Steps

1. **Project Setup and Guidelines**: Check out the [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for detailed setup instructions and contribution guidelines.  
2. **Choose a Topic**: Browse our [Issues section](https://github.com/neopilot-ai/neoai-ide/issues) tagged with `hacktoberfest`. NeoPilot allows multi-assignment!  
3. **Submit Your Demo**: Build and submit your project following the steps in the `CONTRIBUTING.md`.  
4. **Share Your Project**: Post your project on Twitter/X tagging `@NeoPilot` and adding the hashtag `#NeoPilotHacktoberfest` at the bottom of the post. The projects with the **most likes** will be crowned winners!
